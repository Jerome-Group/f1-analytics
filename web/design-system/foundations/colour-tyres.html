Pirelli's own colours, drawn the way the sidewall is marked: a ring with the compound letter inside. The letter is the second channel — hard white and an unknown grey are close at 32 px, and the ring alone would not separate them.

Tyre age

M 14 fresh when fitted
H 21 fitted with laps on it

Age is the tyre's, in laps, and it is not the number of laps run in the Stint. A set fitted with laps already on it is marked, because the two numbers diverging is exactly the case where a single number would mislead.
