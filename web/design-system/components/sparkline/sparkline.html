Complete run — last twelve laps

The dotted rule is the Driver's own best, so the shape is read against something. The head of the run is marked in the team colour, which is the only place the team colour appears inside a cell — it says which row you are looking at without a second glance leftwards.

Laps the feed never sent

The x axis is the lap number, never the laps that happen to have data. So three missing laps keep their width, the run breaks rather than interpolating across them, and the absence is drawn on the floor as a dotted stub. Joining the two ends would invent a value; leaving the space out would quietly rewrite the race.

What may be drawn here

Per-lap values only — lap time, Gap, and pace against tyre age. A per-second trace redraws several times a second for every one of twenty Drivers, which is the cost the frequency tiering exists to avoid. An opened Driver is the one place a live trace is affordable, because there is only one of them.
